    How the plan works

    2core spreads zones across nights automatically; no starting dates are needed. Editing a zone keeps its next watering date; other zones keep their rhythm. Turn seasonal zones off to omit them while keeping their settings. Rebalancing requires reviewing and confirming the date changes. A rain hold postpones exposed zones until the next clear night and shifts their rhythm from that watering. Zones marked “Water during rain delays” keep their normal rhythm. Twice a week alternates 3- and 4-day gaps. This is a preview: saved preferences do not change controller schedules or bypass its rain delay.
